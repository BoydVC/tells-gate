# How a plant eats sunlight

A plant cannot walk to the kitchen, so it makes its own food. Why? It has no mouth and no stomach, so it builds sugar inside its leaves instead.

The leaves catch sunlight with a green stuff called chlorophyll. How? The chlorophyll soaks up red and blue light and uses that energy to split water into tiny pieces.

The plant also pulls in air through little holes under each leaf. It mixes a gas from that air with the pieces of water and makes sugar, and the sugar feeds the whole plant.

You can test this at home with two small plants. Put one on a sunny windowsill and one in a dark closet, give both the same water, and check them after a week.

In conclusion, a plant needs sunlight because the light gives its leaves the energy to turn water and air into the sugar it lives on.
