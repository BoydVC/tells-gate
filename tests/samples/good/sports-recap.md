# Harbor City 27 - 20 Riverside

Harbor City led 14 - 10 at the half and never gave the lead back. The score that put the game away was not a touchdown, but a field goal from 46 yards with two minutes left.

Harbor City's second half, in order: TD. FG. Safety. The touchdown came with a two-point try, so the three scores added up to 13.

Riverside got 10 points after the break, and its last drive ended in the safety at its own 2-yard line.

If you missed the finish, don't wait for the replay to find out who made the stop. It was the backup linebacker, who started because of an injury in warmups.

The team plays at home next Saturday.
